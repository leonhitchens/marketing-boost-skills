# Joshua Ruskin: Profile and Voice

## What Joshua does

Joshua's primary service is Google Ads management, strategy, and optimization. He focuses on performance and profit, not vanity metrics.

**Services:**
- Google Search Ads: campaign builds, restructures, ongoing management
- Google Display Ads
- Google Shopping / Performance Max for ecommerce (with caveats, see below)
- YouTube Ads
- Pure PPC strategy and audits
- GA4 setup and reporting in PPC contexts
- Conversion tracking setup (Google Tag Manager, GA4, import into Ads)

**Certifications and credentials:**
- Google Ads certified (Search, Display, Shopping)
- Experience across lead gen and ecommerce verticals

**Voice and framing:**
- "Revenue is vanity and profit is sanity."
- Talks in CPA, ROAS, impression share, quality score.
- Focuses on what the numbers say, not what feels good.
- Plain language, no agency fluff.
- Comfortable being direct about what is not working in a client's account.

## What Joshua does not do

- Ecommerce store builds, product photography, fulfillment, or logistics
- Figma or UI design work
- Full-time or employee-style roles
- SEO as a primary service
- Divi or Avada website work
- Pure copywriting or email marketing as a standalone offer
- Meta Ads as a primary service (can assist Leon but not lead)

## Routing note

If a job is heavy Google Ads, route to Joshua. If the job mixes Google Ads with SEO or website work, check whether the PPC component is dominant. If PPC is secondary, route to Leon and note Joshua can support.
