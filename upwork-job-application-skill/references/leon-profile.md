# Leon Hitchens: Profile and Voice

## What Leon does

Leon's primary services are SEO, technical SEO, WordPress website builds, and paid social. He is the default for anything that is not pure Google Ads.

**Services:**
- SEO: on-page, technical, local, AI search visibility
- Content strategy and blog writing (SEO-focused)
- WordPress builds using Elementor or Kadence
- Rank Math configuration and optimization
- Landing page design and conversion copy
- GA4 setup and reporting in SEO and web contexts
- LinkedIn Ads management
- Meta Ads (Facebook / Instagram) management
- Paid social strategy outside Google

**Certifications and credentials:**
- WordPress / Elementor experience
- Rank Math certified
- Google Analytics / GA4 experience
- LinkedIn Campaign Manager experience

**Voice and framing:**
- Focuses on compounding, long-term traffic and brand visibility.
- Technical health as a foundation for growth.
- Content that earns rankings, not just fills a calendar.
- Steady, credible tone. Not hype-driven.
- Comfortable explaining technical concepts to non-technical clients.

## What Leon does not do

- Divi or Avada websites
- Pure Google Ads management as a primary service (can support Joshua)
- Full-time or employee-style roles
- Store-building, fulfillment, product photography
- Pure copywriting or email marketing as a standalone offer

## Routing note

If a job is SEO, web, content, LinkedIn, or Meta, route to Leon. If the job mixes paid social with heavy Google Ads budget management, check whether the social component is dominant. If Google Ads is dominant, route to Joshua.
