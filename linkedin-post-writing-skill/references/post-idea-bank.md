# Post Idea Bank

50+ post prompts from Leon's WunderContent Ideas Bank, organized by track, plus the poll bank,
Janine's thought-leadership topics, and lead-magnet hooks. Adapt freely. The point is to spark a
real, specific, uniquely-Leon post, not to copy these verbatim.

When several of these reference one of Leon's real stories, pull the full version from
`story-bank.md`.

## Expert Track (bite-sized expertise for John/Corey)

- Guiding principle: "You build things first, then hire people to do things." Why agencies struggle
  when they skip this. What to ask yourself before you're ready to hire smartly.
- Guiding principle: "Never trust one thing." How diversifying (revenue streams, not relying on one
  employer/client) helps John and Corey manage risk. (Leon's dad's lesson.)
- 3 mistakes to avoid when acquiring an agency. Tell the story of the deal that fell through because
  the owner was the single point of failure, then bailed once he got paid.
- The shift from working to owning: what to look out for. "There is no off." The mental load no one
  warns you about.
- Debunk a market myth: "Everyone tells you X, but I find Y works instead." Make this a repeating format.
- What Google Ads data (or any metric) actually tells you. Could be a series. What to ask your agency
  to get the best results.
- Margin mistakes we've made that you can avoid when scaling your agency.
- Finding the right person for your team feels impossible. Share a story or a tip.
- Paid Media / digital marketing trends that genuinely interest Leon, and what they mean for growing
  an agency.
- Marketing events attended / podcasts listened to: which speaker inspired you and why, plus takeaways.
- What Leon's learned running his own businesses. What he wishes he'd started sooner.
- How to build a thoughtful lifestyle business. One emotional insight: the big contracts don't solve
  everything, they create more headaches. Freedom usually ties back to money, but a thoughtful
  lifestyle business is possible.
- "Sometimes folks do wild things." The employee who tried to walk client data to a competitor.
  A relatable learning about processes and trust.
- Conversion / ROI opportunities businesses miss when they don't track digital performance properly.
- Lessons for starting/growing an agency (regular segment): "We knew nothing. Doing the work, building
  the business, doing sales, all at once. We started hiring a little beyond us so we could work ON it,
  not just IN it."

## Poll Bank (ready to adapt)

## Janine's Thought-Leadership Topics (Expert Track, bigger swings)

Put yourself in John's and Corey's shoes when writing these. Use real case studies where possible;
if not, take the broad insight-driven angle (Leon's a former journalist).

Entrepreneurial ecosystem:
- The rise of pint-size startups: opportunity or challenge? Future-proofing lean operations.
- Fundraising in a tough economy: securing investment without giving up control.
- Scaling smart: the risk of prioritizing client growth over your own business.

Digital marketing & branding:
- Is generative AI stifling creativity? Balancing AI efficiency with human-led creativity.
- Cutting through the noise: carving out a distinct brand voice in a crowded market.
- The value of looking beyond your own industry. Cross-industry learning as an edge.
- The digital landscape: today's key challenges and what's working now.

Sector deep-dives (when relevant):
- CPG's branding dilemma: can design and marketing keep up with shifting loyalty and private label?
- Cleantech's funding crisis: a setback for the green transition?

When drafting any of these, ask: What questions will John and Corey have? What questions SHOULD they
be asking (sharing those makes the post genuinely useful)? Is there a next step to offer? How do I
make it uniquely Leon with a real expression or story?
